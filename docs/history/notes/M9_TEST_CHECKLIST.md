# M9 manual test checklist

1. Press `+ Заглушка` in the encounter sidebar.
2. Create a hero with name, HP, AC, initiative modifier, and several saves.
3. Confirm it appears only on the right and does not create a library entry.
4. Open it in the center and confirm HP/AC/initiative/saves match the form.
5. Roll one of its configured saves; an empty save must stay disabled.
6. Change its HP, press F5, and confirm the state survives.
7. Start combat with both saved-statblock combatants and stubs; they must sort in one initiative order.
8. Press Next through both kinds of combatant and confirm the round wraps normally.
9. End combat and confirm stub HP and initiative reset exactly like statblock combatants.
10. Delete a library statblock and confirm unrelated stubs remain in the encounter.
