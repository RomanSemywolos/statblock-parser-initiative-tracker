# M10 manual test checklist

1. Open a saved statblock and press `Налаштувати картку`.
2. Confirm this mode closes normal text editing and vice versa.
3. Toggle AC off and confirm all encounter copies of that statblock stop showing AC.
4. Toggle saves off and confirm the save grid disappears from its encounter cards.
5. Re-enable both built-ins.
6. Select a trait such as `Magic Resistance`; it should appear as a compact line on every encounter copy.
7. Hover a long custom line and confirm the full text is available.
8. Edit the selected block text in normal edit mode and confirm the card shows the updated text without re-selecting it.
9. Merge away a selected block and confirm the encounter card does not crash if the old ID becomes stale.
10. Press F5 and confirm the card configuration survives through `SavedStatblock` persistence.
11. Confirm HP and initiative remain visible/interactive regardless of card configuration.
